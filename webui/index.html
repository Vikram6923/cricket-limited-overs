Match or series

Two saved teams play one match or a series.

Tournament

Round robin (optionally in two groups), then knockouts. Pick the teams.

Classic series

A nation over a range of years, e.g. India 2007–2011. The squad is their most-capped players of that period, rated on those years; the captain picks the best XI for each match. Ratings are relative to each era, so teams from different years meet on equal terms in the conditions chosen below.

Classic tournament

Nations from any years play a tournament. Each row is one team.

League season

The latest season of a franchise league with its real squads (everyone who played for each team that season), in that league's conditions: double round robin, then IPL-style playoffs (Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, Final). The captain respects the overseas limit.

Interruptions as often as in real matches in the host country (the league's home country).

Fantasy draft

Teams take turns (snake order) picking 15 players each from the pool of an era, rated on those years. Then the squads play a league. The format and conditions below apply to both.
