Match or series

How it works

A match or a best-of series against a friend on another device on the same Wi-Fi, with teams you each pick or squads you draft against each other. Each of you captains a side; when you bat you set your batters' aggression, when you bowl your bowler's plan, both at once, and you see each other's choice after the ball. The host computer must run python web_ui.py --lan.

Host a match
Set the format and conditions below and press Start to open a room. Each player picks their own team in the room.
Draft: take turns picking 15 players each from one era (computer teams can join and make it a league). Set the years in the room.
A series stops as soon as it is decided. You can change it in the room.
Out of time: the bars stay as they were. Other decisions get 60 seconds, then the computer's choice.
Join a match

Round robin (optionally in two groups), then knockouts. Pick the teams.

How it works

A nation over a range of years, e.g. India 2007–2011. The squad is their most-capped players of that period, rated on those years; the captain picks the best XI for each match. Ratings are relative to each era, so teams from different years meet on equal terms in the conditions chosen below.

How it works

Any season of eleven franchise leagues (IPL, BBL, PSL, CPL, SA20, ILT20, BPL, LPL, MLC, T20 Blast, Super Smash) with the real squads (everyone who played for each team that season), in that league and year's conditions: double round robin, then IPL-style playoffs (Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, Final). The captain respects the overseas limit (the most overseas players real XIs fielded that season). IPL from 2023 uses the Impact Player rule.

How it works

An IPL-style auction: ₹120 crore purse, squads of 18–25 (max 8 overseas), retentions and Right to Match cards, players in sets with base prices, an accelerated round for the unsold. Bid live for your team (the computer shows what it would bid), or let the computer bid. Then the squads play a league, and you can captain your side.

Everyone who played that season goes into the auction, rated on their careers; the league is played in that season's conditions, at the franchises' home grounds.

How it works

Run a franchise league season after season, starting from a real season's squads. Between seasons players age (age curves fitted from real careers), have a new season's form, retire, and newcomers arrive: the players who really debuted in the league that year, then made-up youngsters once the real seasons run out. Every few seasons there is a mega auction with retentions; in between, mini auctions where teams keep or release players. Play each season (captain your side if you like) and build up honours and career records.
